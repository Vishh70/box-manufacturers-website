How to Choose the Right Corrugated Box

Home/Blog/Choosing the Right Box
📅 January 15, 2026•📖 8 min read•Guide

Choosing the right corrugated box is one of the most important decisions a business can make for its product packaging. The wrong box leads to product damage, higher shipping costs, and unhappy customers. The right box protects your products, optimizes shipping costs, and even enhances your brand perception.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through everything you need to know about selecting the perfect corrugated box for your specific needs.

Understanding Corrugated Board Structure

Corrugated board is made up of a fluted (wavy) layer sandwiched between flat paper liners. The number of layers determines the "ply" count:

  • 3 Ply (Single Wall): One fluted layer between two liners. Ideal for lightweight items, e-commerce shipping, and retail packaging. Bursting strength: 8–12 kg/cm².
  • 5 Ply (Double Wall): Two fluted layers with three liners. Perfect for heavier items, electronics, and fragile products. Bursting strength: 14–20 kg/cm².
  • 7 Ply (Triple Wall): Three fluted layers with four liners. Used for industrial-grade packaging, machinery, and export shipments. Bursting strength: 20–30 kg/cm².

Choosing the Right Flute Profile

The flute (the wavy middle layer) comes in different sizes, each offering different benefits:

  • A Flute (5mm): Excellent cushioning. Best for fragile products and stacking.
  • B Flute (3mm): Good crush resistance. Ideal for die-cut boxes, shelf displays, and e-commerce mailers.
  • C Flute (4mm): Most common. Good balance of cushioning and stacking strength. Used for shipping boxes.
  • E Flute (1.5mm): Thin profile with excellent printing surface. Perfect for retail packaging and printed boxes.
  • BC Flute (double wall): Combines B and C for maximum protection. Used in 5-ply heavy-duty boxes.

Determining the Right Size

The ideal box size should be only slightly larger than the product it holds (2–3 cm clearance on each side for cushioning material). Oversized boxes lead to higher dimensional weight shipping charges, products shifting and getting damaged, and unnecessary material costs.

"A box should fit your product like a glove — with just enough room for protection, but no wasted space."

GSM Rating: Understanding Paper Quality

GSM (Grams per Square Meter) indicates the weight and quality of the paper used:

  • 100–120 GSM: Standard quality, suitable for lightweight items and cost-sensitive applications.
  • 150–180 GSM: Premium quality with better printability and strength.
  • 200+ GSM: Heavy-duty applications, export packaging, and high-value products.
